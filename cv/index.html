About

I am a Digital Game Design student at Beykoz University, where my academic journey has been honored with multiple Rector's and Dean's Certificates for high achievement. My passion for creation goes beyond code; it started with a lifelong love for writing and storytelling.

In the industry, I've gained experience as a Level Designer for Match-3 titles and as a Developer on projects ranging from 2D pixel-art multiplayer football games to awareness-driven bullet hells. As the Founder & President of the Bumedya Media Club, I lead a creative community producing fanzines, podcasts and digital content.

My curiosity also leads me into the depths of criminal psychology and security, which fuels my interest in building more complex and immersive narratives. Whether I'm working with Unity, Unreal Engine, or a simple pen and paper, I am dedicated to crafting worlds that leave an impact.
